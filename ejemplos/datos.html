    La consulta detrás

    Conversión de proforma a orden, por semana

    SELECT semana,
       COUNT(DISTINCT p.id)  AS proformas,
       COUNT(DISTINCT o.id)  AS ordenes,
       ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(DISTINCT o.id)
             / COUNT(DISTINCT p.id)) AS conversion
FROM proformas p
LEFT JOIN ordenes o ON o.proforma_id = p.id
GROUP BY semana
ORDER BY semana;
